## v2.0.6 - 2026-08-16
### Added
* Customer invoice emails via Resend when invoices are ready, overdue, paid, or fail
* Account billing Subscriptions tab to view Dashboard-created subscriptions, toggle autopay, pick a payment method, and cancel at period end
* Email magic-link sign-in via Resend, with Google still visible and GitHub behind more options
* Notify when a customer saves their first payment method, and after an in-app invoice payment offer to save the method, turn on autopay, or switch to bank
* Invoice payment can use a saved card or bank account through Stripe Payment Element
### Changed
* Replace invoice card surcharges with dual pricing: list price is the card price, bank payments earn 2% off, and invoices pay through Stripe's native confirmation secret
* Stop auto-defaulting the first saved card so a newly added bank can be opted in as the default for 2% off
* Book covers fall back to the WordPress External Featured Image URL when there is no media-library thumbnail
* Account avatar invites guests to join billing and signed-in users to open their account. Sign-in is a spacious no-password card that lands Join on the billing portal, with warmer magic-link copy and an autopay next step when there are no invoices.
* Refresh book review pages from WordPress at least every 60 seconds
* Billing uses the site’s centered page layout, clearer copy, and pending tab feedback while invoices and payment methods load.
### Fixed
* Allow Next.js builds to collect Stripe routes when STRIPE_SECRET_KEY is unset at compile time
* Billing sign-in honors the invoice return URL, prefills the billed email, warns on the wrong account, and rematches empty Stripe customers
* Keep email magic-link sign-in out of the billing client bundle so Next can build
* Stop expanding Stripe subscription products five levels deep so the payment methods page can load
* Check-your-email keeps the return path, draft cache, and invoice email when using a different address.
* Magic-link sign-in emails render on Vercel instead of failing on a missing React Email renderer.
* Account tabs highlight immediately on click instead of looking broken until the next page finishes loading.
